How I work

Four steps,
every time.

1

Understand the purpose

What needs to happen, who needs the information and what a useful result looks like.

“Who reads this, and what do they decide?”
2

Organize the details

Bring the information together, spot the gaps and give the work a clear structure.

“What’s missing, and who has it?”
3

Build something practical

A report, process, presentation or website that people can understand and use.

“Can someone new follow this?”
4

Refine and follow through

Check the details, take in feedback and keep the next steps visible until it’s done.

“Who’s doing what, by when?”
